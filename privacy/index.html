01. Who this policy covers

British Midland Group is a hobby flight-simulation community governed by a management committee. This draft describes personal information used for British Midland Virtual, British Midland Regional and BMAcars, and the shared British Midland Group systems supporting this flight simulation community. It is about virtual aviation services, not the operations of real-world airlines.

Responsible person identified by management: Richie. Full name, formal controller identity and contact details remain to be confirmed before this notice is finalised. “British Midland Group” is used here as the community name; it does not establish a separate legal entity.

BMG supplies European Cargo Virtual with the shared tracking and PIREP framework. Management confirms that ECV pilot information is handled in the same way as British Midland pilot information. European Cargo Virtual is included here in relation to its use of those shared systems. Group branding, shared management or shared technology does not by itself establish that European Cargo Virtual has the same data controller. Its controller, any separate privacy notice, and the responsibilities for shared data must be confirmed before this draft is finalised.

02. Information used

Pilot accounts and membership
Pilot identifiers (such as BMA0001), nicknames or display names, internal account identifiers, membership role and status, join dates, and flight statistics. Sign-in uses credentials and authentication records. The management portal uses an internal email-format login identifier; it is not a personal email address supplied by the pilot. Registration collects Discord identity, a chosen nickname and an assigned BMA pilot ID, rather than requesting a real name or personal email address. Pilots use their assigned BMA ID to sign in.
Discord account linking
Discord user IDs linked to pilot IDs, account-linking tokens, and information supplied during registration or support. Pilots apply using /register in Discord. After management approval, the bot sends initial login credentials by private Discord direct message. The website’s linking form sends the entered BMAcars password and private linking token to the registration service to verify ownership. Never post your password or private link in a Discord channel.
Flights and BMAcars
Flight numbers, routes, aircraft and registrations, simulator positions and track timestamps, flight progress, flight reports (PIREPs), flight time, fuel use, landing performance, scores and review records. Simulator coordinates describe a simulated aircraft’s position, not your physical location.
Requests and service use
Information you choose to include in support or privacy requests, including your email address if you contact us by email. Website and service providers may also process IP addresses, browser/device information, request times and diagnostic logs when serving requests. The exact log inventory and retention require confirmation.

Information comes from you, linked community accounts, flight activity submitted through BMAcars, and management’s administration and review of that activity. The current website repository does not establish every field collected by the desktop client or Discord service; that inventory remains subject to review.

The joining page specifies membership for people aged 18 or over. The age-checking procedure and handling of inadvertently collected children’s data need confirmation. Do not submit identity documents or sensitive personal information unless a verified, appropriate process asks for them.

03. Purposes and lawful bases

Flight data is collected to generate and maintain PIREPs for realistic virtual-airline flight simulation. Account and flight information also supports membership, sign-in, account linking, flight history, statistics, management review and support. Public operations displays allow visitors to follow community flights.

Lawful bases below are proposals for controller review, not confirmed legal determinations. Each purpose must be assigned and documented before publication; acknowledging this notice is not blanket consent.

Accounts, linking and core flight services
Assess contractual necessity only if there is a valid membership agreement and the processing is objectively necessary to deliver it. Otherwise assess legitimate interests in administering the community, including necessity and the impact on members.
Public pilot names, IDs and flight tracking
Assess legitimate interests in sharing community flight activity, including reasonable expectations, the risk of identification, less intrusive alternatives and objections. If consent is chosen instead, establish a genuinely optional choice and a working withdrawal process before relying on it.
Security, moderation, support and operational review
Assess legitimate interests in protecting accounts, preventing misuse, resolving requests and maintaining accurate flight records. Document the balancing assessment and limits on access.
Legal duties and optional features
Use legal obligation only where a specific applicable duty requires the processing. Use consent where required for optional processing or non-exempt device storage/access, with a clear way to withdraw it. Do not treat continued browsing or an acknowledgement checkbox as consent.

04. Public pilot details and tracking

The public Operations page is designed to display pilot IDs and pilot names supplied by the public flight data, alongside flight details. Live aircraft markers, simulator flight tracks and recent operations can be viewed without a management login. A nickname may still identify you, especially when linked to your Discord identity or other profiles.

Public information can be copied or captured by visitors. Removing an item from this website cannot guarantee removal of copies held elsewhere. Consider what your display name reveals about you.

Management screens also use pilot statistics, roles, status, detailed flight reports and review information. Their presence behind a sign-in screen does not establish that database access controls have been audited. BMG has agreed a proposed Discord-bot preference that will replace both the display name and pilot ID with “Private Pilot” on public displays, while retaining aircraft and route information. Implementation and verification are pending; this draft does not claim the preference is currently available. Hiding public identity will not delete the account or underlying flight records.

05. Shared systems and service providers

British Midland Group systems
Shared operational tools support pilot administration, flight reporting and management. BMG management reviews British Midland PIREPs. ECV managers currently have portal access to British Midland records, although they do not review them. Restricting access to what each role needs remains a review item, not a completed change. Shared infrastructure does not authorise unrestricted sharing.
Supabase
The website uses Supabase for authentication, database requests and the account-linking service. Its records support pilots, flight reports, operations and management access. The COO has confirmed that the primary project region is West EU (Paris, France). Backup availability and retention, contractual roles, subprocessors and deletion arrangements remain to be verified.
Discord
Joining and registration are directed through Discord, and the management tools link Discord user IDs with pilot accounts. Discord also operates its own platform under its own privacy terms. The bot delivers initial credentials by private DM after approval. Its permissions, stored messages and backend handling still need a separate service review.
SimBrief
SimBrief is required for flight planning. Pilots enter their SimBrief identifier into BMAcars, which management confirms saves it locally on their device. The identifier is used for the flight-planning integration; exact requests and any planning data retained with PIREPs remain to be verified. Local storage does not mean an identifier is never transmitted to a provider. SimBrief/Navigraph’s own terms apply to its service.
VATSIM
A VATSIM ID is optional. Pilots may fly and submit PIREPs without one. When provided, it is used to associate a PIREP with the pilot’s VATSIM identity for flight review. Management confirms it is saved locally in BMAcars; whether it is also included in submitted PIREPs remains to be verified.
Website infrastructure
The source references Google Fonts, jsDelivr, unpkg and cdnjs for fonts or libraries, and OpenStreetMap tiles for the live map. Loading those resources sends network requests to those providers, which can receive your IP address and request metadata. Confirm the production host and its logging practices as part of the final inventory.

International processing

Service providers may process information outside the UK. The primary database region is Paris, France; other processing locations, recipients and applicable transfer safeguards for the actual service configurations remain to be confirmed. These must be confirmed, including how a person can obtain information about the safeguards, before publication. This draft makes no promise of UK-only storage.

06. How long information is kept

PIREPs currently remain in the database, and deletion is handled on request. Management has specified that departed members are marked inactive for two months and, if they do not return, their accounts are then anonymised. For explicit deletion requests, management has set a target of completion within 28 days of receipt by the privacy email, once that address is supplied. The two-month inactivity period does not apply as a waiting period for explicit deletion requests. These processes still require implementation verification. BMG intends to preserve genuinely anonymised flight records for virtual-airline history and statistics after applicable deletion requests.

The proposed retention criteria are the time needed to administer membership, provide requested flight history, resolve disputes or reviews, protect services, and meet a specific applicable legal duty. These criteria must be converted into an approved, implemented schedule for accounts and Discord links, PIREPs and statistics, raw flight tracks, registration tokens, support messages, security logs and backups.

BMG’s intended account-deletion process closes the entire shared pilot account, covering British Midland, Regional and shared ECV access, and removes membership from both British Midland and ECV Discord servers. Identifying links must be removed from any retained flight records. This workflow still requires implementation verification, including backup handling. Removal from a Discord server does not itself erase previous messages or bot notifications. Retention reviews must also address unnecessary personal information without waiting for a request. Management reports that retained tracking points do not identify individual pilots. The website associates points with an active flight identifier, so any indirect link from flight records to pilots must still be checked before the tracks can be described as anonymous. Hiding a flight from the map is not evidence that the underlying track has been deleted. Pseudonymous pilot IDs remain personal information where they can be linked back to a person.

07. Your rights and choices

Depending on the applicable law and the basis used, you may ask for access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, erasure, restriction of processing, and portability of qualifying data. These rights have conditions and exceptions; deletion is not automatic in every case.

Your right to object

You can object, on grounds relating to your situation, to processing based on legitimate interests, including any public flight display relying on that basis. The controller must consider the objection under the applicable rules. You have an absolute right to object to direct marketing, if undertaken.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. An acknowledgement that you have read this policy does not waive your rights.

You may also raise concerns about automated decisions where applicable. Flight scores and review screens exist; their effect on membership and the extent of human review must be confirmed before any statement about legally or similarly significant automated decisions is made.

Use the contact section below to identify the relevant account and request. Only proportionate information needed to verify identity should be requested. Requests must be handled within the applicable statutory time limits, generally one month under UK GDPR, subject to permitted extensions and rules on clarification and identity checks.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office through the ICO complaints service, or to your local data protection authority where applicable. You do not have to complete an internal complaint first.

08. Cookies and browser storage

Cookies and similar technologies include browser local storage. Supabase’s browser authentication can persist a signed-in session using local storage; the management pages use its standard client configuration. Clearing that storage or signing out can affect access to protected features.

The linking page reads a private token from the URL fragment and removes it from the visible address before submitting it to the registration service. The privacy page itself adds no analytics, tracking scripts or consent cookies.

No analytics or advertising integration was identified in the reviewed website code. This is not a claim that every deployed service is cookie-free: hosting, third-party resources and desktop/Discord services require a production audit. Record the names, providers, purposes and lifetimes of storage in use. Any non-exempt storage or access must have the required consent controls before activation.

You can manage cookies and site data through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may prevent sign-in. A privacy acknowledgement is separate from cookie consent.

09. Security and account safety

The reviewed website uses HTTPS service endpoints, Supabase authentication, management access checks and a restricted content security policy on the linking page. These observations are not an assurance that every backend permission or organisational safeguard has been verified.

The COO has confirmed that direct Supabase administration is limited to them and that MFA is enabled on their administrative account. This does not imply that all portal users have MFA. Management must still verify least-privilege access, database policies, credential handling, staff access reviews, backup protection and incident-response procedures before making wider security claims. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Pilots cannot currently change their BMAcars password themselves; a password-change/reset process remains a planned security improvement. Keep passwords and private linking URLs confidential, sign out on shared devices, and report suspected account misuse through a verified management channel. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or unnecessary identity documents with a privacy request.
