Draft notice — review in progress

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email Contact.britishmidlandvirtual@gmail.com. This draft is available for transparency; the remaining legal and operational review items must be resolved before it is treated as final.

01. Who this policy covers

British Midland Group is a community-run flight-simulation group governed by the British Midland Group management committee. The committee collectively determines why and how pilot information is used for British Midland Virtual, British Midland Regional, BMAcars and the shared systems supporting European Cargo Virtual. It is the responsible decision-making body for the processing described in this notice. European Cargo Virtual is operated by the same committee.

This notice concerns virtual aviation services, not real-world airlines. British Midland Group is the community name, not a claim that a separate company has been incorporated. Privacy enquiries can be directed to the committee through the contact details below. Our administration team handles privacy matters; no formal Data Protection Officer has been appointed.

02. Information used

Pilot accounts and membership
Pilot identifiers (such as BMA0001), nicknames or display names, internal account identifiers, membership role and status, join dates, and flight statistics. Sign-in uses credentials and authentication records. The management portal uses an internal email-format login identifier; it is not a personal email address supplied by the pilot. Website pilot applications collect a display name, numeric Discord user ID, optional VATSIM CID and optional SimBrief pilot ID. They also record acknowledgement of this notice, its version, an age-18-or-over confirmation, application state, timestamps and management review decisions. No personal email address is requested for an application. Supabase uses a generated internal identifier such as bma0042@auth.britishmidlandvirtual.internal; pilots use their assigned BMA ID to sign in. Optional IDs supplied here are retained with the application and do not by themselves verify ownership or change existing ACARS settings.
Discord account linking
Discord user IDs linked to pilot IDs, account-linking tokens, and information supplied during registration or support. Pilots apply through the website join form and management reviews website applications in the Management Portal. The legacy /register flow remains available during rollout until the replacement has been tested. After management approval and account creation, the bot sends initial login credentials by private Discord direct message. Website registration does not use Discord OAuth. Management must confirm the intended recipient before approving; a typed Discord ID alone does not establish identity. The website’s linking form sends the entered BMAcars password and private linking token to the registration service to verify ownership. Never post your password or private link in a Discord channel.
Flights and BMAcars
Flight numbers, routes, aircraft and registrations, simulator positions and track timestamps, flight progress, flight reports (PIREPs), flight time, fuel use, landing performance, scores and review records. Simulator coordinates describe a simulated aircraft’s position, not your physical location.
Requests and service use
Information you choose to include in support or privacy requests, including your email address if you contact us by email. Website and service providers may also process IP addresses, browser/device information, request times and diagnostic logs when serving requests. The exact log inventory and retention require confirmation.

Information comes from you, linked community accounts, flight activity submitted through BMAcars, and management’s administration and review of that activity. The current website repository does not establish every field collected by the desktop client or Discord service; that inventory remains subject to review.

The joining page specifies membership for people aged 18 or over. The age-checking procedure and handling of inadvertently collected children’s data need confirmation. Do not submit identity documents or sensitive personal information unless a verified, appropriate process asks for them.

03. Purposes and lawful bases

We use account and flight information to administer membership, provide sign-in and account linking, maintain flight reports and statistics, review flights, support pilots and protect the community. Public flight information supports community recognition and identifies who completed each flight.

The committee has confirmed these purposes. The legitimate-interests assessments remain to be completed and documented; this draft does not represent a completed legal assessment.

Legitimate interests — community administration and security
The proposed basis for necessary membership administration, flight history, account protection, moderation and support is our legitimate interest in running a safe and functioning virtual-airline community. The committee must assess necessity and balance these purposes against pilots’ rights and reasonable expectations.
Legitimate interests — public pilot identification
Chosen nicknames and pilot numbers are displayed to recognise participation and identify who completed each flight. Public identification is intended as a standard membership feature, not an optional consent-based feature. Before finalising reliance on legitimate interests, the committee must assess whether identifying pilots publicly is necessary and proportionate, including less intrusive alternatives. Pilots retain the right to object; a joining requirement does not remove that right.
Contract
Membership terms have not yet been created. This notice therefore does not claim contractual necessity as an established basis. Any future reliance on contract must relate to processing objectively necessary to fulfil a valid agreement and must be explained to pilots before the change.
Consent
If a genuinely optional feature relies on consent, we will request a separate, specific choice and explain how to withdraw it. Agreement to join, continued browsing and acknowledgement of this notice are not blanket consent. Mandatory public identification is not described as freely given consent.

04. Public pilot details and tracking

The public Operations page displays pilot numbers and chosen nicknames supplied by the public flight data, alongside flight details. Pilots are not required to use their real names. Live aircraft markers, simulator flight tracks and recent operations can be viewed without a management login. A nickname may still identify you, especially when linked to your Discord identity or other profiles.

Public information can be copied or captured by visitors. Removing an item from this website cannot guarantee removal of copies held elsewhere. Consider what your display name reveals about you.

Management screens also use pilot statistics, roles, status, detailed flight reports and review information. Their presence behind a sign-in screen does not establish that database access controls have been audited. The committee’s current policy is that chosen nicknames and pilot numbers appear publicly as a standard membership feature for community recognition and identifying who completed a flight. There is no verified group-wide Private Pilot setting. Pilots may raise an objection through the privacy contact, and the committee must consider it under the applicable rules. Hiding public identity does not delete the account or underlying flight records.

05. Shared systems and service providers

British Midland Group systems
Shared operational tools support pilot administration, flight reporting and management across British Midland and ECV. The committee has confirmed that all its members, including the ECV manager, require access to pilot records across both airlines for shared management oversight. Access is for committee duties, not unrelated personal use. Role permissions and access reviews still require technical verification; committee membership is not a claim that all underlying permissions have been audited.
Supabase
The website uses Supabase for authentication, database requests and the account-linking service. Its records support pilots, flight reports, operations and management access. The COO has confirmed that the primary project region is West EU (Paris, France). Backup availability and retention, contractual roles, subprocessors and deletion arrangements remain to be verified.
Discord
The website application collects a Discord user ID for welcome delivery and account association, and the management tools link Discord user IDs with pilot accounts. Discord also operates its own platform under its own privacy terms. The bot delivers initial credentials by private DM after approval. Its permissions, stored messages and backend handling still need a separate service review.
SimBrief
SimBrief is required for flight planning. Pilots enter their SimBrief identifier into BMAcars, which management confirms saves it locally on their device. The identifier is used for the flight-planning integration; exact requests and any planning data retained with PIREPs remain to be verified. Local storage does not mean an identifier is never transmitted to a provider. SimBrief/Navigraph’s own terms apply to its service.
VATSIM
A VATSIM ID is optional. Pilots may fly and submit PIREPs without one. When provided, it is used to associate a PIREP with the pilot’s VATSIM identity for flight review. Management confirms it is saved locally in BMAcars; whether it is also included in submitted PIREPs remains to be verified.
Application security checks
When website applications are open, the join form loads Cloudflare Turnstile to help prevent automated submissions. Cloudflare receives requests from your browser, and the registration service sends the resulting challenge token to Cloudflare for validation. The service stores HMAC-derived Discord identifiers with hourly attempt counters for rate limiting, rather than raw contact details in those counters. Application records themselves retain the submitted Discord user ID. Management must maintain the rate-limit cleanup job and confirm the retention schedule for application and decision records.
Website infrastructure
The source references Google Fonts, jsDelivr, unpkg and cdnjs for fonts or libraries, and OpenStreetMap tiles for the live map. Loading those resources sends network requests to those providers, which can receive your IP address and request metadata. Confirm the production host and its logging practices as part of the final inventory.

International processing

Service providers may process information outside the UK. The primary database region is Paris, France; other processing locations, recipients and applicable transfer safeguards for the actual service configurations remain to be confirmed. These must be confirmed, including how a person can obtain information about the safeguards, before publication. This draft makes no promise of UK-only storage.

06. How long information is kept

The committee has approved the following retention rules. Implementation, including deletion jobs, leave handling and backup treatment, still requires verification. This notice records the agreed schedule; it does not claim that automated closure or anonymisation is already operating.

New pilots and flight inactivity
The inactivity clock starts when an account is approved until the first accepted flight. Each accepted flight then resets the clock. Approved leave of absence pauses the inactivity clock for the approved period. After 60 days of inactivity, the closure and anonymisation process begins and must be completed within a further 28 days: up to 88 days in total, excluding approved leave.
Leaving the group
If a pilot leaves both airlines, including leaving both associated Discord communities, a 60-day grace period begins before closure and anonymisation starts. That process must finish within the following 28 days. Leaving one airline or Discord community does not by itself close the shared account while the pilot remains a member of the other airline; the flight-inactivity rules still apply. The departure rule does not require immediate account closure.
Explicit deletion requests
Requests sent to Contact.britishmidlandvirtual@gmail.com are handled separately, with a target of completion within 28 days of receipt. There is no 60-day waiting period for an explicit request. Rights remain subject to applicable conditions and exceptions; any justified retention or permitted extension will be explained to the requester.
Shared accounts and retained flight history
Closure of the entire shared account covers British Midland, Regional and shared ECV access. BMG intends to retain only genuinely anonymised flight records for community history and statistics after applicable deletion or closure. Anonymisation must remove identifying links, not just replace a nickname. Pilot numbers and flight identifiers remain personal information where they can be linked to an individual.

The complete process must also address authentication records, Discord links and bot notifications, public displays, PIREPs, statistics and linked flight tracks. Removing Discord membership or hiding a public entry does not itself erase messages, notifications or underlying records. Backup deletion and restoration safeguards still need verification.

Separate retention periods or meaningful criteria for unsuccessful applications, registration tokens, support correspondence, security logs and backups remain under review. Retention reviews must remove unnecessary personal information without waiting for a request. These outstanding categories are not automatically covered by the pilot-account timetable above.

Public pilot roster

The public roster displays British Midland membership number, chosen public name or existing nickname, airline rank, membership status, flight count and block hours, most recent arrival date (UTC), and a count of VATSIM flights completed in the rolling last 60 days. Existing nicknames are used; real names are not collected for this feature. Recorded flights count unless rejected by management. VATSIM flights are identified by the ATC provider stored with each flight report.

The roster can be read by anyone, including visitors without an account, and public information may be copied or indexed by others. It does not publish account UUIDs, Discord, SimBrief or VATSIM identifiers, login information, review notes. The roster adds no sign-in or browser storage.

Contact management privately at Contact.britishmidlandvirtual@gmail.com to change your public name or ask to be removed. A roster-specific visibility control removes your entire row, including membership number and statistics. This control does not yet hide existing Operations pages, historical notifications or third-party copies; there is no verified group-wide Private Pilot control.

The purpose is community recognition and identifying pilot participation. The legitimate-interests assessment for public membership statistics remains outstanding. The committee must explain the feature to members and handle objections; the agreed flight-account retention rules are described above. The wider policy remains a draft; this technical update does not establish a lawful basis or constitute consent.

Public events

Managers can choose to show an ACARS event on the public Events page. Published summaries include the event title, description, scheduled time, route, aircraft, organiser and cancellation status. Managers should include only information suitable for public viewing in those fields. Events with past scheduled start times remain in the public archive while website visibility is enabled.

Joining events takes place in ACARS. The public page does not expose attendee lists, pilot identifiers, event creator accounts, private briefings or briefing links. Management can hide an event from both the upcoming list and archive; edits and cancellations appear when the page refreshes, normally within one minute. Deleting an event in ACARS also removes it from the website. Hiding or deleting a public entry cannot remove copies already made by third parties.

07. Your rights and choices

Depending on the applicable law and the basis used, you may ask for access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, erasure, restriction of processing, and portability of qualifying data. These rights have conditions and exceptions; deletion is not automatic in every case.

Your right to object

You can object, on grounds relating to your situation, to processing based on legitimate interests, including any public flight display relying on that basis. The controller must consider the objection under the applicable rules. You have an absolute right to object to direct marketing, if undertaken.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. An acknowledgement that you have read this policy does not waive your rights.

You may also raise concerns about automated decisions where applicable. Flight scores and review screens exist; their effect on membership and the extent of human review must be confirmed before any statement about legally or similarly significant automated decisions is made.

Use the contact section below to identify the relevant account and request. Only proportionate information needed to verify identity should be requested. Requests must be handled within the applicable statutory time limits, generally one month under UK GDPR, subject to permitted extensions and rules on clarification and identity checks.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office through the ICO complaints service, or to your local data protection authority where applicable. You do not have to complete an internal complaint first.

08. Cookies and browser storage

Cookies and similar technologies include browser local storage. Supabase’s browser authentication can persist a signed-in session using local storage; the management pages use its standard client configuration. Clearing that storage or signing out can affect access to protected features.

The linking page reads a private token from the URL fragment and removes it from the visible address before submitting it to the registration service. The privacy page itself adds no analytics, tracking scripts or consent cookies.

No analytics or advertising integration was identified in the reviewed website code. This is not a claim that every deployed service is cookie-free: hosting, third-party resources and desktop/Discord services require a production audit. Record the names, providers, purposes and lifetimes of storage in use. Any non-exempt storage or access must have the required consent controls before activation.

You can manage cookies and site data through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may prevent sign-in. A privacy acknowledgement is separate from cookie consent.

09. Security and account safety

The reviewed website uses HTTPS service endpoints, Supabase authentication, management access checks and a restricted content security policy on the linking page. These observations are not an assurance that every backend permission or organisational safeguard has been verified.

The COO has confirmed that direct Supabase administration is limited to them and that MFA is enabled on their administrative account. This does not imply that all portal users have MFA. Management must still verify least-privilege access, database policies, credential handling, staff access reviews, backup protection and incident-response procedures before making wider security claims. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Pilots cannot currently change their BMAcars password themselves; a password-change/reset process remains a planned security improvement. Keep passwords and private linking URLs confidential, sign out on shared devices, and report suspected account misuse through a verified management channel. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or unnecessary identity documents with a privacy request.

10. Contact and policy updates

Data Protection Officer & Responsible Parties

British Midland Group is a community group and does not have a formally appointed Data Protection Officer. Our administration team handles data protection matters and privacy concerns. For data protection enquiries, complaints or requests to exercise your rights, please contact us privately using the email address below.

Privacy contact email

Contact.britishmidlandvirtual@gmail.com

Send privacy questions, complaints and requests to exercise your data protection rights, including deletion requests, to this address. The COO and CEO jointly take responsibility for answering privacy requests and complaints. Access to this mailbox is restricted to them. They act on behalf of the British Midland Group management committee.

Privacy complaints will be acknowledged within 30 days. The COO and CEO will make appropriate enquiries, keep you informed and communicate the outcome without undue delay. This complaints acknowledgement period is separate from the deletion-request target and statutory rights-request deadlines.

Please send requests privately by email rather than posting personal records in public Discord channels. We may need proportionate information to verify account ownership before acting on a request.

A useful request identifies your pilot ID or linked account, the service concerned, and what you would like to access, correct, remove or challenge. Do not include your password.
