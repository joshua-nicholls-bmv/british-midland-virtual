Our home

Wolverhampton
Halfpenny Green

EGBO

Nestled in the heart of the West Midlands, Wolverhampton Halfpenny Green Airport (EGBO) is the proud home of Midland Aero Club.

Surrounded by the beautiful countryside of Staffordshire and Shropshire, EGBO provides the perfect starting point for General Aviation adventures. From scenic flights over the Shropshire Hills to exploring the Welsh borders and beyond, the airfield offers endless opportunities for recreational flying.

Originally established as an RAF airfield during the Second World War, Halfpenny Green boasts a rich aviation heritage and a welcoming General Aviation atmosphere.

Airfield Information

  • Main Runway: 16/34 — 1,170 m (Asphalt)
  • Secondary Runway: 10/28 — 1,055 m (Asphalt)
  • Crosswind Runway: 04/22 — 646 m (Asphalt)
  • Elevation: 283 ft AMSL
  • Radio: Halfpenny Green Information — 123.005 MHz
  • Facilities: AFIS, fuel, aircraft parking, hangarage and café.

With its excellent facilities and picturesque surroundings, Halfpenny Green is the ideal home for our growing fleet of light aircraft.

All six club aircraft are permanently available at EGBO, giving members the freedom to explore at their own pace.
