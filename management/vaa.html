Flight evidence

How qualification is calculated

  • A retained member pilot record, a numeric CID, VATSIM provider, a server-verified session and positive observed online duration are required. A typed CID or VATSIM label alone is not evidence.
  • The observed network callsign must match the planned callsign and begin BMA or BMR. Both prefixes must be included in your registered VATSIM callsign list.
  • Departure and arrival must be four-letter ICAO codes. Off-blocks, takeoff, landing and stand timestamps must be valid and chronological. Completion means the stand timestamp, not the PIREP submission date.
  • Submitted, Completed, Approved or Accepted reports are eligible only with completed flight timestamps and no outstanding management review. Other statuses are excluded.
  • Repeated PIREP IDs, verification sessions and matching pilot/start/callsign combinations cannot increase the count. CID syntax checks do not independently validate ownership; identity evidence comes from the server verifier.
  • Observed seconds demonstrate sampled network participation, not full-flight coverage. Last verification status is exported even when the pilot disconnected after earlier valid observations. Historical reports without evidence are not retroactively verified.

CSV files contain pilot identifiers and flight evidence. Keep them within authorised management or share deliberately with VATSIM for the application/audit. Exporting does not submit anything automatically.
