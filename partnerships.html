OFFICIAL TECHNOLOGY PARTNER

SayIntentions.AI

Our official partnership with SayIntentions.AI brings AI air traffic control, intelligent dispatch and customised cabin crew to your British Midland flights.

As part of the SayIntentions.AI Virtual Airline Programme, British Midland pilots can bring our airline’s identity into their simulator experience, from flight preparation to the cabin.

Make it a British Midland flight

In your SayIntentions Pilot Portal, open the Virtual Airline settings and select British Midland Virtual to link your airline.

Thinking of subscribing? Speak to our staff team on Discord before purchasing to ask about the current British Midland pilot offer and any applicable conditions.
