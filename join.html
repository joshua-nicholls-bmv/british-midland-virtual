WHY BRITISH MIDLAND

Built Around Modern Airline Operations.

British Midland Virtual combines realistic flying, modern technology and a friendly community to create an authentic airline experience from the Heart of Britain.

Mainline and Regional Fleet

Choose from Airbus mainline aircraft, the Fokker 100, ATR 72 and Embraer E190.

Custom BM ACARS

Record your flights and follow your progress with our own ACARS platform.

Routes to Explore

Choose a short regional sector, a European service or a long-haul flight.

Friendly Community

Whether you're new to flight simulation or an experienced captain, everyone is welcome.

GETTING STARTED

Four Steps To The Flight Deck.

Follow these steps to join the airline and prepare your first flight.

01

Apply on Our Website

Complete the application below. Management reviews your details, then your approved BMA login arrives by Discord welcome DM.

02

Download BM ACARS

Install our custom ACARS client directly from Discord and connect your simulator to British Midland Virtual.

03

Choose A Route

Select a scheduled service from our network and prepare your aircraft for departure.

04

Start Flying

Log your first flight, earn your statistics and become part of the British Midland Virtual community.
