GETTING STARTED

Four Steps To The Flight Deck.

Everything begins in our Discord. From there you'll gain access to BM ACARS, our resources and everything you need for your first British Midland flight.

01

Join Discord

Meet the community, receive announcements and access everything you'll need to get started.

02

Download BM ACARS

Install our custom ACARS client directly from Discord and connect your simulator to British Midland Virtual.

03

Choose A Route

Select one of our scheduled services from Birmingham and prepare your aircraft for departure.

04

Start Flying

Log your first flight, earn your statistics and become part of the British Midland Virtual community.
