WHY BRITISH MIDLAND

Built Around Realistic Flying.

British Midland Virtual recreates the experience of operating a modern airline from Birmingham Airport. Every flight is supported by realistic procedures, an expanding Airbus fleet and a friendly community that enjoys flying together.

Professional Operations

Fly scheduled services using realistic procedures, dispatch information and operational standards designed around a genuine airline experience.

Modern Airbus Fleet

Operate our growing fleet across Europe and beyond, with aircraft, routes and operations continually expanding.

Friendly Community

Fly alongside experienced and new pilots alike in an environment built on professionalism, respect and support.

Growing Airline

Join a virtual airline that is actively evolving with new destinations, partnerships, technology and events.

YOUR JOURNEY

Four Steps To The Flight Deck.

Getting started with British Midland Virtual takes just a few minutes. Once you're connected, you'll be ready to begin flying from Birmingham and progress through our expanding network.

1

Join Discord

Become part of our community, meet other pilots, receive support and stay informed with the latest British Midland Virtual news.

2

Install BM ACARS

Download the British Midland ACARS client to track your flights, record statistics and connect directly to our operations platform.

3

Choose Your Route

Browse our route network, select your aircraft and prepare for departure from Birmingham to destinations across Europe.

4

Start Flying

Complete your first flight, build experience and become part of the next chapter in British Midland Virtual's story.
