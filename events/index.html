Join through ACARS

Select Join in ACARS on an event to open it in the app. Allow the browser prompt, sign in and register your attendance.

  1. Open ACARS

    Sign in to your pilot account and open the Events tab.

  2. Choose your event

    Find the event by its title and check the route, time and briefing.

  3. Register attendance

    Select Register Attendance in ACARS. You can manage your attendance there too.
